About

Operations meets digital

Turning shop-floor problems into simple, deployable web applications — from data entry to predictive insight.

I work at the intersection of manufacturing operations and software. My focus is building lightweight, self-contained tools that teams can actually use on the plant floor — no heavy infrastructure, just clean data entry, clear reporting and measurable results.

From OEE and SPC analytics to a full CMMS and cross-department task tracking, each solution is designed around real operational workflows: availability, performance, quality, downtime and Kaizen-driven improvement.
